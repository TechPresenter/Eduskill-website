# Image Sources & Licensing

Attribution and licence record for every third-party image used on the
EDUSKILL INDIA FOUNDATION website.

## Licence summary

All photographs below are from **Unsplash**, used under the
[Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use for commercial and
non-commercial purposes, no permission or attribution required. Attribution is
recorded here anyway as good practice and so any image can be traced to source.

> **Illustrative imagery.** These are stock photographs used to illustrate the
> themes of our work. They are **not** photographs of EDUSKILL INDIA FOUNDATION
> programmes, staff, beneficiaries or events, and must not be captioned or
> presented as though they were. Replace them with genuine programme photography
> as it becomes available.

## Processing applied

Every image is downloaded at source resolution, resized to a web-appropriate
width (never upscaled), converted to **WebP**, and saved with a descriptive
filename. Originals are not committed.

## Team & testimonial avatars (own artwork, not third-party)

| Files | Section |
|---|---|
| `uploads/team/avatar-*.svg` (4) | Team member cards |
| `uploads/testimonials/avatar-*.svg` (4) | Testimonial cards |

Generated locally: an initial monogram on a brand-palette gradient, ~535 bytes
each, with the person's name as the SVG `aria-label`. Honorifics are stripped,
so "Dr. Neha Gupta" yields "NG". No third-party imagery, no invented identity.

## Images deliberately NOT replaced with stock photography

These slots are intentionally left without stock imagery. Recorded here so the
decision is not mistaken for an oversight.

| Section | Rows | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Team members | 4 | Real, named individuals. Attaching a stock stranger's face to a named person misrepresents them. Neutral initial-based avatars are used until real photographs are supplied. |
| Testimonials | 4 | Same — a testimonial attributed to a named person must not carry an unrelated stock portrait. |
| Partner logos | 5 | A partner's logo is their trademark; it cannot be substituted or invented. Supply the real logos via the admin panel. |
| Sponsor logos | 4 | As above. |
